A finance and accounting leader with over 13 years of experience in high-growth technology and SaaS companies. Over that time, I have built accounting departments from the ground up, managed multi-entity consolidations across international subsidiaries, and implemented core ERP and reporting systems.

My approach to controllership is simple: financial records require exact mathematical accuracy, clean segregation of duties, and audit-ready workpapers. While language models are helpful for analyzing text and researching variances, core ledger math belongs in deterministic code with human review and approval controls.

That is the foundation behind TrueRec AI. I built it to demonstrate how accounting teams can eliminate close bottlenecks safely, using rule-based calculations for ledger matching and cash proofs, while applying AI strictly to assist with exception analysis under full human oversight.

From managing multi-jurisdiction close workflows to serving as the primary lead for Big 4 external audits and lender debt facilities, I focus on building finance infrastructure that scales predictably while keeping teams audit-ready every day of the month.
